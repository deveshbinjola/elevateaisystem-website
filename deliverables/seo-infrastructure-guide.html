Section 04

What You Need To Do (Human-Required)

We built it. Now you activate it. Five critical actions that take 30 minutes total.

Action 1 of 5

Submit Sitemap to Google Search Console

Why: Google won't crawl your site faster or better just because you submit your sitemap. But it will index new pages more quickly and with higher priority if you do.

Action 2 of 5

Submit Sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools (Powers ChatGPT Search)

Why: Bing's index powers ChatGPT's search results. If your sitemap isn't in Bing, you're invisible to every conversation where ChatGPT is asked to search for something.

Action 3 of 5

Turn Off "Block AI Bots" in Cloudflare

Why: If Cloudflare is blocking AI crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, Applebot, PerplexityBot), your site won't be indexed by any AI model. Security > Bots > Enable AI crawlers.

Action 4 of 5

Set SSL/TLS to "Full (strict)" in Cloudflare

Why: HTTPS is required for schema markup to work properly. Google and AI crawlers won't trust your structured data without full encryption.

Action 5 of 5

Collect Client Testimonials (Enables Review Schema)

Why: Review schema turns testimonials into rich snippets in search results. One 5-star review with schema can increase CTR by 20-30%.
