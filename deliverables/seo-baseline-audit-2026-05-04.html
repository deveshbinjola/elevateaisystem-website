What's already working

Don't fix what isn't broken. These signals are solid across the board.

Solid

robots.txt covers every major LLM crawler

GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, anthropic-ai, CCBot, OAI-SearchBot, Cohere, Bytespider all explicitly allowed. AEO/GEO foundation is strong.

Solid

Security headers configured

HSTS, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy all set in _headers. Image caching aggressive at 30 days.

Solid

www canonical and clean URLs

Apex 301 to www in _redirects. Pretty URLs without .html for marketing pages. No duplicate content split.

Solid

Blog system has AEO bones

blog_skill.md already has the 5-Zone AI-citable framework, "One Question 100 Answers" strategy, and built-in JSON-LD. Ahead of most competitors.

Solid

0 image alt-text gaps

Across 110 pages, every <img> has an alt attribute. Accessibility floor is high.

Solid

Brand OS funnel pages have schema

brand-os.html, build-your-brand.html, augmented-coach.html have JSON-LD with proper offer/price fields. Money pages are AEO-ready.
