Section 02

Your First 7 Days: Timeline

Here's exactly what we do together to get your Content OS running.

Day 1

Kickoff Call

We extract your voice, your positioning, and your ICP. You bring 3 examples of content you love (yours or others') and 3 you hate. We record your speaking patterns, your phrases, what makes you uniquely you. 45 minutes. This is the foundation everything else is built on.

Day 2

Voice Calibration

We build your Brand Voice Profile — the exact tone, phrases you use repeatedly, phrases you'd never say, your go-to stories, your analogies. We document this in a living guide. This ensures every piece of content sounds like you, not a robot.

Day 3

Positioning Lock

We define your one problem, your transformation statement, your pillar question for month 1. This is the core message that runs through everything. Every post answers this question in a different way.

Day 4

Content Architecture Setup

We configure Growth/Nurture/Conversion categories. Your 7-day calendar is locked. Publishing schedule is set. Platforms are connected. All systems ready for content generation.

Day 5

First Batch Generated

Your first week of content is ready for review. 30 pieces across Instagram, LinkedIn, email, blog, YouTube. You can see exactly what you'll be publishing before it goes live.

Day 6

Feedback Round

You review everything. Mark what sounds like you and what doesn't. Flag any tone misses, messaging adjustments, content you want re-written. We refine based on your feedback.

Day 7

Go Live

Your content starts publishing across all platforms. Your weekly rhythm begins. Monday newsletter in, content out by Tuesday. The engine is running.

Section 04

What We Need From You

To get your Content OS running smoothly, we'll need these seven things from you. Fill these out before Day 1.

  • Access to your newsletter archive — Beehiiv, Substack, ConvertKit, or Revue. We extract your voice from past newsletters.
  • 3 examples of content that sounds like you — Posts, articles, or videos you've created (or admire) that capture your voice
  • 3 examples of content you dislike — What NOT to do. Types of posts that don't feel authentic to you.
  • Your top 5 client questions — What do people ask you in DMs, emails, and calls? What problems do they bring to you?
  • Your best client result — With numbers if possible. The transformation you're most proud of.
  • Social media logins or publishing access — Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube. Or Meta Business Suite / scheduling tool access (Buffer, Later, etc.)
  • Your preferred review method — Google Doc, Notion, email, or Slack? How you want to give feedback on content.

Section 06

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what every Content OS client asks.

What if I don't have a newsletter yet?

We help you set one up. It takes 30 minutes. Your newsletter is the fuel for your Content OS engine — without it, we can't extract your voice at scale. But don't worry, we'll walk you through everything.

What if I don't like something?

Flag it in your review. We refine until it sounds like you. This is why Day 6 exists — we iterate on feedback. By the time content goes live on Day 7, it feels authentic.

How long until I see results?

Most clients see engagement lift in week 2–3 (comments, shares, saves going up). Lead flow typically starts showing in 30–60 days. Content is a compounding asset — it takes time, but it works.

Can I edit the content before it posts?

Yes. You get a review window every week. You can approve, request changes, or ask us to re-write. The goal is for you to feel 100% confident in what's going live.

What happens if I miss a Monday?

We use your previous week's content and audience research to keep the engine running. You don't miss a beat. But ideally, Monday becomes your new power move — 5 minutes of input for a week of content output.

Can I pause for a month?

Yes. You can pause anytime. Just give us 48 hours notice so we don't generate content you won't use.

What platforms does Content OS cover?

All of them. Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, YouTube, email, blog. We generate for every major platform. You choose which ones to publish to.

Do you handle scheduling and publishing?

We generate and optimize the content. You approve it. For publishing, you can either give us access to your scheduling tool (Buffer, Later, Meta Business Suite) or handle it yourself. Your choice.
