10 AI skills that handle your core workflows. Each one is a reusable prompt you'll reference again and again.

Skill 01

Personal Writing Editor

What it does: Takes any draft (email, post, proposal, article) and refines it to match your voice, tone, and quality standards. Knows your authentic phrases and avoids anything that sounds corporate or off-brand.
How to use:
  1. Paste the prompt into Claude/ChatGPT
  2. Paste your draft
  3. Get back a polished version in your voice
Example use case: You drafted a LinkedIn post but it feels stiff. Paste it into this skill. Get back something that sounds like you said it yourself.
Skill 02

Email Drafter (5 Modes)

What it does: Generates ready-to-send emails in 5 modes: cold outreach, follow-up, delivering bad news, negotiation, appreciation. Input the situation and recipient. Get back a complete email that sounds like you.
How to use:
  1. Choose your mode (e.g., "cold outreach")
  2. Paste the prompt

  4. Get a draft you can send immediately
Example use case: You need to follow up with a prospect you met 3 weeks ago. Input their name, company, and what you discussed. Get back a warm, authentic follow-up that doesn't sound pushy.

Section 03

How To Use Your Skills

Your skills work the same way every time. Once you learn the flow, you'll move fast.

5-Step Process

  1. Step 1: Open any AI tool — Claude recommended (best results with your calibrated prompts)
  2. Step 2: Copy the full prompt — From your skill file (.md format)
  3. Step 3: Paste it as your first message — Just the prompt, nothing else
  4. Step 4: Follow the prompt's instructions — It will tell you exactly what input it needs
  5. Step 5: Get your output, refine if needed — Most outputs are production-ready. Tweak if needed for your specific context.

Pro Tips

  • Claude + These Prompts = Best Results: Claude understands your voice calibration best. Use Claude as your primary AI for these skills.
  • Batch Your Skills: Run multiple pieces through the Personal Writing Editor at once. Run content idea generation weekly, not daily.
  • Save Your Favorites: Bookmark the skills you use most. Skill #3 (Repurposer) and Skill #6 (Content Idea Generator) tend to be workhorse skills.
  • Iterative Refinement: First output good? Send it. First output close? Ask the AI to refine one more round. No output is perfect on round one.
