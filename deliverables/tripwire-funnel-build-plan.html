The Closed Loop

Two events. One number. One review cadence.

A loop is closed when the outcome of step N feeds back into step 1. The two events below are the spine. Without both, you have a leaky funnel, not a loop.

Event 1 of 2
type tripwire_purchased fires Stripe webhook on $7 success fields email, source, utm_*, brand_os_session_id, created_at writes to cp_tripwire_purchases + cp_funnel_events
Event 2 of 2
type cohort_enrolled fires when $7 buyer pays cohort fee fields email, tripwire_purchase_id, days_between, created_at writes to cp_funnel_events (joined view)

Friday 9am review (recurring)

  • $7 buyers this week (count + sources)
  • $7 → $2K conversion rate this week vs trailing 4-week
  • Median time-to-conversion (days between events)
  • Top-converting source (UTM)
  • One change to ship next week (page copy, email cadence, source mix)
  • If conversion < 6% for two weeks straight, kill or pivot the product
