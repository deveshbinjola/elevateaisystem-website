Step 3: The "One Question, 100 Answers" Method

Pick one question your ideal clients ask repeatedly. Answer it 100 different ways across every format — blog, thread, video, carousel, case study, framework diagram. Each answer adds a new angle, a new example, a new data point. This is how you become the default authority AI models cite when that question gets asked.

Your pillar question this month
"[The #1 question your ideal clients ask you]"
How to find it: Check your DMs for patterns. Look at questions in communities. Notice what people search for in your space. The question that keeps coming back — that's your pillar.

The 3-Line Bio Formula

Section 03

Building Community: The Referral Engine

Authority gets you found. Community gets you trusted. A tight group of 50 engaged people creates more business than 5,000 passive followers.

Identify Your First 20

People already engaging — commenting, sharing, asking questions

Personal Invite (DM)

"I noticed you [specific thing]. I'm building a group of 20. Want in?"

Activate (Day 1-7)

Intros, exclusives, spotlights, member-to-member connections

Grow by Nomination (5-10/month)

"Who's one person you know who should be here?"

Day 1-7 Activation

Make the First Week Matter

Day 1: Introduce everyone (tag + why you picked them)
Day 2: Share something exclusive
Day 3: Ask for real input on a decision
Day 4: Spotlight a member's win
Day 5: Intro two members who should know each other
Day 6-7: Prompts that get people talking to each other

Weekly Rhythm

Contribution Loops

Monday: Ask a question requiring experience
Wednesday: Spotlight one member's contribution
Friday: Share what you learned from the group

Monthly: Co-working session, skill share, mastermind breakout, show-and-tell

Growth Rule

Depth Beats Width — Always

After 30 days, ask your core members: "Who's one person you know who should be here?" Add 5-10 per month maximum. 50 highly engaged people create more referrals, more trust, and more revenue than 500 lurkers. Quality is the strategy.

Section 04

Your 30-Day Launch Sprint

Don't try to do everything at once. Here's the exact sequence — one week at a time.

Week-by-Week Execution Plan

Week 1

Foundation. Define your one problem. Write your positioning statement. Update all bios with the 3-line formula. Identify your first 20 community members. Create 3 pieces of content teaching your methodology.

Week 2

Outreach. DM all 20 people with personal invitations. Create the private group space (Telegram, Discord, or X group). Post 7x on your main platform. Share one real client result or process doc.

Week 3

Activation. Execute Day 1-7 of community activation. Introduce members to each other (3 intros minimum). Post 7x on your main platform. Document one thing that worked and one that failed.

Week 4

Momentum. Facilitate first member-to-member collaboration. Spotlight 2 member contributions publicly. Post 7x on your main platform. Ask your core 20 for their first referrals. Begin pillar question rotation.
