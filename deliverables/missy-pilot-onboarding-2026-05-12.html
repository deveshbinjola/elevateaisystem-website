Why she matters

First female user = first signal

The platform was built on Sunny's male-coach ICP. Missy is the canary. Three things to watch.

Watch #1 · Voice

Does the first caption sound like her?

The acceptance test for the female-voice adaptation. If she says "this doesn't sound like me", iterate the few-shot block. Architecture stays.

Watch #2 · Lead Layer

Does the inbox fill up in 15 minutes?

CSV upload + MailChimp forward. If the platform can't show her a populated inbox the first session, time-to-value fails.

Watch #3 · Copy Hygiene

Where does male-coded language slip through?

"Brothers", "the work", "warrior", "edge", "container". Anywhere Missy sees these in default copy is a defect to log.
