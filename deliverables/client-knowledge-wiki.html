How It's Built

Three Layers. One Brain.

Your wiki has three distinct layers that work together. We set up all three during Build Your Brand.

01

Raw Sources — Your Input Layer

Everything you know, create, or experience gets added here. Immutable. Always the source of truth. You don't organise it — you just add to it.

Voice notes Session transcripts Articles you read Client questions Frameworks you create
02

The Wiki — Your AI-Maintained Brain

LLM-generated markdown pages that summarise, connect, and flag contradictions across everything you've added. The AI reads your sources and writes the wiki automatically. You never write a note again.

Topic pages Client patterns Framework summaries Concept comparisons Cross-links
03

Schema — The Rules Layer

A set of instructions that tells the AI how to maintain everything — what pages to create, how to structure summaries, when to flag contradictions. We write this for you during setup. You never touch it.

Maintenance rules Page templates Update workflows
