Section 05

Your Content Rules

These aren't suggestions. These are the guardrails that ensure every post, email, video, and page sounds like you—not like a template.

Always Do This Never Do This
  • Lead with a real story or specific number
  • Write like you talk (short sentences, natural rhythm)
  • End with a clear next step
  • Reference your own experience and client results
  • Use corporate jargon or buzzwords
  • Start with "In today's fast-paced world..."
  • Use generic stock-photo language
  • Write anything you wouldn't say out loud to a client
Pro Tip

The Out-Loud Test

Before posting or sending anything: Read it out loud. If it sounds weird saying it out loud, it doesn't belong in your content. Your audience can feel authenticity. They can smell when you're being someone you're not.

Writing Framework

The SCQA Framework — For Posts, Emails & Pitches

When you don't know how to open a piece, run it through SCQA. It's the fastest way to go from blank page to compelling story.

S — SITUATION

Set the context your reader already recognises. Make them nod. "Most coaches post every day and hear nothing back."

C — COMPLICATION

The tension. The thing that breaks the situation. "Then the algorithm shifts and everything stops working."

Q — QUESTION

The natural question the reader is now asking. "So what actually works when the old playbook breaks?"

A — ANSWER

Your insight, framework, or POV. Delivered with confidence. "You build a system that doesn't depend on reach."

Use SCQA for LinkedIn posts that need a hook, cold emails that need to earn attention, and sales pitches that need to feel human rather than scripted.
