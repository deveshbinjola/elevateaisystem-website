The thesis

One coach. One stack. No outside money.

The marketing world is loud because it talks to everyone. We don't. The studio writes to one man, a men's embodiment coach doing $10K to $30K, building toward a cohort or a retreat, who already knows the shape of the work and is tired of his brand language not matching what he actually does.

Every venture is built around that person. Every essay, every page, every offer is tested against him. The depth of the position is the moat. The stack is what gets handed over when he's ready.
