# Post: Three People. $500K ARR. Six Months. The Coach Math Just Broke.

---

Fortune dropped a story this week that should change how you plan the next 12 months of your business.

Read this slowly.

The CEO is 23.

He has never written a line of code.

His company is six months old.

It just hit $500,000 in annual recurring revenue.

The team is three people.

The customers are real. Six enterprise contracts across energy, manufacturing, professional services, and finance.

And the second story in the same article was Fathom AI.

$300K ARR in twelve weeks. Gross margins above 90%. Operating costs under 10% of revenue.

Twelve weeks.

Now I want you to look at the team you've been planning to hire.

Not to feel bad. To recalibrate.

Because the math you've been running is from a different decade.

Most coaches I work with have a very specific spreadsheet in their head.

Hit $20K a month, hire a VA. Hit $30K, hire a content person. Hit $50K, hire an OBM.

Eventually you have a team of five. You make $400K a year. You're exhausted.

That math made sense in 2022.

It is now obsolete.

The new math looks like this.

Add agents to scale revenue. Each one costs nothing close to a salary. Output is gated by your strategy, not by team capacity. Margins expand as you grow instead of compressing.

That isn't a tweak. That's a different curve entirely.

The 23-year-old hitting $500K isn't smarter than you. He just isn't protecting himself from the obvious.

Here's the part most coaches miss.

The threat isn't the founder in the Fortune story.

The threat is the 200 quiet coaches in your niche who are doing the same thing without telling anyone yet.

The career coach with an intake agent. The fitness coach whose follow-up never sleeps. The dating coach with a content agent that posts twice a day in their voice.

They aren't louder than you yet.

They're just compounding faster.

And every month they run, the gap gets harder to swim.

So stop planning the team you would have hired in 2024.

Plan the agent stack you would build if you were starting your business today.

One workflow. The most repetitive thing in your week.

Build the agent that runs it.

Run it for 30 days.

Then count what changed.

That's how the new math starts.

Not with a $15M valuation.

With one workflow gone from your plate.

Your move:
