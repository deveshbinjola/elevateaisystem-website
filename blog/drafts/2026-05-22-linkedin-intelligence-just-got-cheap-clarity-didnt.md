A frontier-class AI model shipped this week at $1.50 per million tokens.

Four times faster than the thing that amazed everyone three months ago.

And it beats the more expensive tier sitting right above it.

Most coaches read that and feel a spike of fear.

"It's getting smarter. It's getting cheaper. It's coming for me."

That's the wrong read.

Here's what actually happened.

The price of being smart just collapsed.

For a decade, knowing the framework was your moat. People paid because the knowing was rare.

Now the knowing costs less than your coffee.

If your whole offer is "I know the thing"...

the floor just dropped out from under it.

But watch what got more valuable in the same move.

When intelligence becomes free and infinite, one thing about you becomes priceless.

A point of view.

The model can reason. Write. Plan. Brilliantly. For pennies.

What it cannot do is decide what's worth saying.

It has no stake. No scars. No nine years of getting it wrong first.

It's a perfect, weightless intelligence with nothing it actually believes.

So the bottleneck moved.

It used to be capability. Could you do the thing.

That part just went to zero.

The new bottleneck is clarity.

Having a lens sharp enough that running it through an infinite genius produces something only you would have produced.

The coaches who panic this year will respond by learning more tools.

They'll try to out-know a thing that knows everything.

You cannot win that. You never needed to enter it.

Cheap intelligence isn't your replacement.

If you actually have a point of view, it's the cheapest, fastest, most tireless set of hands your thinking has ever had.

You bring the lens.

It brings the leverage.

The price drop didn't change who wins.

It just removed the place the unclear ones were hiding.

Your move:

If you handed an infinite free genius to your client tomorrow... what would they still need YOU for?

Write the answer in one sentence.

If you can't, that's not a tooling problem.

That's the most important problem you have this year.
