## Post: OpenAI Bought the AI Built for Coaches. Then Killed It Overnight.

---

OpenAI just acquired Convogo.

An AI platform built specifically for executive coaches.

Leadership assessments. Feedback reports. Client prep workflows.

Real coaches had built their entire practice around it.

Then the deal closed.

And the product stopped existing.

Overnight.

This is OpenAI's 9th acquisition in 12 months.

Not one of those acquired products is still running for end users.

The playbook is always the same:

Buy the team. Absorb the IP. Wind down the product. Move on.

And somewhere, coaches are rebuilding their workflows from scratch this week.

...

Here's the uncomfortable truth most people in the coaching space won't say out loud.

The AI tools being built "for coaches" right now...

They're not building for you.

They're building to get acquired.

And you're a casualty of that deal.

...

There are two ways to build an AI stack for your business.

**Option A:** Subscribe to vertical tools someone else controls. Hope they don't get acquired. Hope the pricing doesn't change. Hope the product survives.

**Option B:** Build a system on primitives you own. Use tools as contractors, not employees. When one disappears, you update a config file. Nothing breaks.

Option A is easier to start.

Option B is the only one that survives.

...

Ask yourself this about every AI tool in your stack right now.

"If this disappeared tomorrow... what breaks?"

If the answer is "not much"... you've built something resilient.

If the answer is "everything"... that's the work.

...

The Convogo coaches are rebuilding right now.

You don't have to be one of them.

Your move:

Map your AI stack this week. Identify what you'd lose if any single tool shut down tonight. Then start modularizing.

That's the move.
