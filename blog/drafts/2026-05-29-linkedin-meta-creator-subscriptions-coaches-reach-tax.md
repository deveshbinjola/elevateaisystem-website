Meta just put a price tag on your reach.

$49.99 a month.

That is the new "Meta One Advanced" tier they rolled out yesterday.

The perks?

"Featured in feeds."

"Higher in search results."

That is not a verified badge.

That is paid placement.

The thing you used to earn with content quality, consistency, and an audience that actually wanted to hear from you...

Is now a line item on a credit card statement.

Here is what most coaches will miss.

This was not new.

Your reach has been collapsing for years. Posts that hit 20K in 2022 scratch 2K now. Same voice. Same effort. A fraction of the eyes.

The platform was already taxing you.

It was just taxing you in time. In dread. In analytics tabs you stopped opening.

Now the bill arrives in dollars.

Two camps are forming this week.

Camp A reaches for the card. They pay the $50. They keep posting the same content. They wait for the algorithm to bless them.

They are renting reach.

Camp B reads this news, exhales, and asks the harder question.

What do I actually own?

Email list. Website traffic. YouTube. A voice that lives in places Meta cannot turn off.

One year from now, only one of these coaches has a business.

You probably know which one.

The platform is a megaphone.

Not a body of work.

You build the body of work somewhere they cannot touch.

Your move:

Do not log in today to subscribe.

Log in today to look at the last 90 days of posts and ask one question.

How many of these new leads ended up on something I actually own?

If the answer is "almost none"...

That is your real problem.

The $50 will not fix it.
