# LinkedIn Draft, May 2, 2026

**Post Type:** Zero-click thought leadership
**Voice notes:** Short-line rhythm. Emotional arc. Challenge CTA. No hashtags. No links.

---

An AI agent just bought a domain.

Read that sentence twice.

On April 30, Cloudflare and Stripe shipped an open protocol.

Agents can now do this:

Create accounts.
Buy domains.
Start paid subscriptions.
Deploy apps to production.

By themselves.

You set a $100 a month cap.

The card never leaves your wallet.

The agent gets a token, not a key.

For 18 months, "AI agent" mostly meant "tool that drafts your content."

Notice the verbs.

Draft. Summarize. Follow up.

All the heavy verbs were still yours.

Decide. Buy. Deploy. Pay.

Stripe and Cloudflare just took those too.

This is the part most coaches I work with don't see yet.

They have a spreadsheet in their head.

It says: "When I hit my next revenue tier, I'll hire someone to handle the tech."

Domain. Hosting. CRM. Funnel deployment. Subscription management.

That was a $1,500 a month VA.

Sometimes a $3,000 "tech setup" partner.

It just got reduced to one agent and a $100 cap.

The "I'm not technical" excuse stops working.

Because "technical" just got reduced to "tell the agent what you want."

Here is the part that bothers me.

Most coaches won't avoid this because it's hard.

They'll avoid it because letting an agent spend their money feels like losing control.

It isn't.

The protocol literally caps it at $100 a month.

The card stays in your wallet.

What you're protecting isn't control.

It's the identity of being the one who clicks all the buttons.

Until something else holds the credit card...

You are the credit card.

Your move:

Pick the most repetitive thing on your plate this week.

The one that involves logging into something, clicking forms, and paying for something.

That thing is your first agent.

Describe it. Set the cap. Hand it off.

The 23-year-old hitting half a million in ARR with three people isn't smarter than you.

He just stopped protecting himself from the obvious one cycle earlier.

What workflow are you about to hand off?
