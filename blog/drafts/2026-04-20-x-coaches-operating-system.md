The 2026 coaching business has four layers.

Most coaches only know two.

1. Visibility (SEO, AEO, GEO, LLMO)
2. Operations (lead capture → retention)
3. Agents (autonomous AI running ops)
4. Resonance (you... the product)

Miss a layer and something obvious breaks.

---

ALT VERSION (contrarian hook):

"How much traffic do I need?"

Wrong question.

Traffic isn't the bottleneck.

Your operations layer is.

You can't pour water into a broken cup and wonder why you're dry.

Fix the cup.

---

ALT VERSION (build order):

Most coaches build their business backwards.

Agent first. Then funnel. Then maybe figure out positioning.

The correct order:

1. Resonance
2. Operations
3. Agents
4. Visibility

Traffic is never the bottleneck when the rest is broken.

You just drown faster.

---

ALT VERSION (ladder):

Resonance only: $0-10K/mo

+ clean ops: $10-25K

+ agents: $25-50K

+ visibility compounding: $50-100K

Full stack humming: $100K+

Each layer unlocks the next rung.

You don't skip them.

---

ALT VERSION (mistakes):

Three ways coaches break their own stack:

1. Automating chaos (agents scale broken processes, not fix them)

2. Hiding in pretty ops (no resonance underneath)

3. Buying tools instead of building systems

A subscription isn't a marketing system.

A chatbot isn't an intake system.

A system is: when X happens → Y runs → Z gets notified.
