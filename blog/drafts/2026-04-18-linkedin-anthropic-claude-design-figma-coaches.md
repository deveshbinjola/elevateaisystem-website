# LinkedIn Draft — April 18, 2026
## Post: Anthropic Just Made Figma Optional. Coaches, Pay Attention.

---

Anthropic shipped a new product yesterday.

Claude Design.

Slides. Decks. Mockups. Prototypes. Lead magnet covers. Sales one-pagers.

All from a prompt.

Same day, Figma stock dropped 8%.

$1.5 billion in market cap... evaporated in an afternoon.

The market understands what most coaches haven't caught up to yet.

The "I need to learn Figma" era is ending.

...

Most coaches I work with are paying for some combination of Figma, Canva Pro, Beautiful.ai, Gamma, and a designer or VA on retainer.

Add it up.

Most are at $200 to $800 per month on visual tools alone.

That's $2,400 to $9,600 per year.

For a category that just became optional.

...

Here's the part nobody is saying out loud.

This is the third creator tool category that AI just absorbed in 60 days.

Writing tools got eaten when Claude and ChatGPT became default for first drafts.

Coding tools got eaten when Claude Code went mainstream.

Design tools got eaten yesterday.

Video editing. Lead capture. CRMs. They're next.

...

Coaches who built their workflow around a specific tool... lose hours every time a category falls. Re-learning. Re-templating. Re-onboarding.

Coaches who built their workflow around what they want as an output... just point Claude at it.

That's the difference between renting infrastructure and owning a system.

...

Three moves for this week.

Move 1. Audit your design line items. Every visual tool you pay for. Add it up.

Move 2. Test Claude Design on the next thing you would have built in Figma or Canva. Pitch deck. Lead magnet. Carousel. Compare honestly.

Move 3. Don't rage-quit your tools tomorrow. Build a parallel workflow over 30 days. Let the cheaper tool win.

...

Don't fall in love with tools.

Fall in love with outcomes.

The tools change.

The outcome you want for your client doesn't.

Your move:

Open Claude this weekend. Ask it to design something for your business. Then look at your tool bills with fresh eyes.

You'll know what to do.
