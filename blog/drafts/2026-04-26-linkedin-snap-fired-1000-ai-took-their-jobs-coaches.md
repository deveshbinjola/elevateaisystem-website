Snap fired 1,000 people last week.

AI is now writing 65% of their code.

The stock went up 8% the same day.

Let that land.

A company cuts 16% of its workforce because machines are doing most of the work...

And investors reward them for it.

This isn't a scandal.

It's a signal.

Most coaches I work with see stories like this and think:

"That's a tech company. That doesn't apply to me."

Here's the uncomfortable truth.

Consulting firms just cut junior hiring by 40%.

Not because they're struggling.

Because AI is doing the junior work.

Think about your coaching business for a second.

How much of your week is actual coaching...

...and how much is the work around the coaching?

The follow-up emails.

The intake forms.

The onboarding sequences.

The content production.

The admin that bleeds into your evenings.

That work is your execution layer.

And AI can handle every piece of it.

The coaches who are building right now aren't replacing their coaching.

They're replacing everything that happens before and after the call.

Same transformation.

Way leaner operation.

3x the capacity.

The threat isn't AI replacing you.

The threat is a coach in your niche who figured this out six months ago.

Your move:

Pick the one task in your business that's most repetitive and most draining.

Not the transformation work.

The stuff around it.

Build an AI layer for that one thing.

Let it run two weeks.

Then look at what you did with the time you got back.

That's how the operating model changes.
