Everyone keeps asking the wrong question.

"Is AI going to replace me?"

40% of the work now runs with AI doing part of it.

Up from 18% a year ago.

So far, the story you'd expect.

Then look closer.

In the same twelve months...

The top 20% of freelancers saw their income climb 12%.

The bottom 40% watched theirs fall 23%.

Same platform.

Same tools.

Same AI sitting there for everyone.

The machine didn't lower the average.

It tore the market in half.

Here's the part that should keep you up.

The people getting wiped out aren't the bad ones.

They're the average ones.

The reliable "I'll build the thing you describe" crowd.

That was a real living for a decade.

Now a business owner opens a chat window and gets a 7-out-of-10 version in nine seconds, for the price of a coffee.

Good enough at a fraction of the cost.

That's the most dangerous sentence in business right now.

It doesn't come for the worst work.

It comes for the middle.

So what did the top 20% do to get a raise?

They didn't out-type the machine. Nobody can.

They stopped selling the thing it now does for free.

And started charging for the thing it can't touch.

Not the doing.

The deciding.

Which strategy fits this business. Which version is actually right. What to throw out. What the founder is too close to see.

That isn't labor.

That's judgment.

And judgment got more valuable the second labor got cheap.

Your move:

Open your offer. Read it with one question.

Am I charging for the doing... or for the deciding?

The fork is already here.

You don't get to skip it.

You only get to choose which side you're on when it widens.
