603 solopreneurs surveyed.

89% now use AI in their business.

Read that again.

89%.

Today it makes you the room.

The study isolated the top earners. $150K+, five-plus years in business.

They don't just use AI more. They embed it deeper. Five or more business functions. Content, research, admin, follow-up, marketing. Running. Not "sometimes used."

And then the twist:

77% of them prioritize in-person networking.

The most AI-leveraged people in the study are also the most human.

That's not a contradiction.

That's the playbook.

AI flooded the internet with competent content. So trust collapsed back to the oldest channel there is...

A real person. In a real room. With real presence.

The machine runs the busywork.

The human does the connecting.

Your move:

Under five? You just became average.

Then open your calendar and count the real rooms you'll be in this month.

Zero? The machines aren't your problem.
