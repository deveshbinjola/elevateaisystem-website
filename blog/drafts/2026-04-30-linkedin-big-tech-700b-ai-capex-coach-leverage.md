# Post: Big Tech Just Pledged $700 Billion to AI Infrastructure. Coaches, This Is Your Leverage Window.

---

Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta, and Amazon reported earnings last night.

Read this number out loud.

Seven hundred billion dollars.

That's their combined 2026 AI capex.

Last year it was $410 billion.

In twelve months, the world's most disciplined capital allocators decided to spend an extra $290 billion on the same thing.

Not on marketing.

Not on acquisitions.

Not on stock buybacks.

On servers, GPUs, data centers, fiber, power.

On the floor underneath your business.

Most coaches I talk to are reading this kind of headline like spectators.

"Cool. Big tech stuff. Not for me."

That sentence is the most expensive thing you can say in 2026.

Because every dollar of that $700 billion eventually flows downhill.

Cheaper inference. Bigger context windows. Better voice agents. Smaller, sharper open-source models.

Persistent memory. Multi-day workflows. Agents that hold a conversation across weeks the way a senior team member would.

This is the floor your competition is about to stand on.

The 24-year-old coach launching today isn't smarter than you.

She just gets to start her business on top of a $700 billion floor that didn't exist 18 months ago.

Old coach math: hire a VA at $30K/mo, a content person at $50K, an OBM at $80K. Stack humans. Margins compress as you grow.

New coach math: deploy an agent for the same workflow. Cost drops from $5K/month to $300/month. Margins expand as you grow.

That equation just inverted. The $700 billion is what made the inversion possible.

The window where the infrastructure exists, the tools are mature, but most coaches haven't moved yet... that is the leverage window.

Right now, you can be three steps ahead of your category by deploying one workflow as an agent.

One.

That window will not be open in 12 months.

Don't read this and feel busy.

Don't open eight tabs of "best AI tools for coaches."

Pick the most repetitive thing in your week. The one that drains 60 to 90 minutes of energy. Build the agent that runs it. 70% is fine. The other 30% is your judgment, applied in a five-minute review.

Run it for 30 days.

Notice what changes.

The hyperscalers placed their bet on April 29.

Your move:

What's the one workflow you'd hand off first if you trusted the agent to do 70%?
