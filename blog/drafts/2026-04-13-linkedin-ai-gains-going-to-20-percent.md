## Blog: 75% of AI's Gains Are Going to 20% of Businesses

---

PwC just dropped a study today.

1,217 executives. 25 industries.

The finding?

74% of all AI economic value is being captured by just 20% of companies.

The other 80% are splitting the leftovers.

Here's the part that hit me...

The top 20% aren't using better tools.

They're not spending more money.

They're not hiring AI engineers.

They're doing one thing differently.

They redesign their workflows around AI.

The bottom 80%?

They just add AI on top of what they were already doing.

Adding ChatGPT to a broken process just gives you a faster broken process.

I see this every week with coaches.

"I use AI to write my captions."

Cool. That's the 80% move.

The 20% coach built a system where AI writes the caption, schedules it, tracks engagement, scores the leads, triggers a personalized email sequence, and books the discovery call.

Same starting point.

Completely different result.

Meanwhile, solopreneurs who redesigned their workflows with AI agent stacks are seeing 340% revenue increases.

With zero increase in working hours.

The gap isn't closing.

It's accelerating.

Your move:

Look at your AI usage this week.

Are you adding tools... or redesigning how your business actually runs?

That's the only question that matters right now.
