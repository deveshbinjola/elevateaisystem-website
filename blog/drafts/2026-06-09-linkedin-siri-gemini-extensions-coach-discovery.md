# LinkedIn Draft — Siri Is Now Gemini (2026-06-09)

Yesterday Apple sold Siri's brain to Google.

$1 billion a year. A custom Gemini model. Running inside the device your clients touch 200 times a day.

And almost every coach I know shrugged.

"Apple news. Not my problem."

It's your whole problem.

Because here's what actually shipped at WWDC...

The question box moved.

For 20 years, your business depended on one assumption. Someone hits a wall at 11pm, opens a browser, types words into Google.

Your website. Your SEO. Your funnel. All built on that.

Starting this fall, that person lifts their phone and just talks.

"Siri, I'm burned out. I need help."

No search results page. No ten blue links. No page two.

One answer. From a model that either knows you exist... or doesn't.

And it gets harder.

iOS 27 lets users swap Siri's brain. Gemini by default. Claude if they want. ChatGPT if that's what they know.

Three different models deciding whether your name comes up.

You can't rank for that.

You can only be so clear, so consistent, and so specific that every model reaches for your name when someone describes your category.

Try it right now.

Open any AI model and ask: "Who should I work with as a [your niche] coach?"

If you're not in the answer, your green SEO dashboard is measuring a game that just ended.

Your move:

Pick one sentence that says exactly who you help. Put it everywhere. Same words. Until the machines can't confuse you with anyone else.
