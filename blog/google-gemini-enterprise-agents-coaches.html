Google Just Put an AI Agent Inside Every Tool You Already Pay For.

The Signal: At Google Cloud Next 2026 (April 22), Google rebranded Vertex AI to Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. No-code agent builder baked into Google Workspace. A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol so agents from different platforms can finally talk to each other. Project Mariner for live web browsing. The tools you have been paying for monthly just became an autonomous AI system.

Most coaches I work with think of AI the same way.

It's a separate tab.

A separate login.

Something you go to.

Google just ended that assumption.

This week at Cloud Next 2026, Google announced something that barely made a dent in the mainstream coaching conversation.

But underneath the rebrand and the press release, something actually significant shifted.

The tools you've been using every day... Gmail, Drive, Docs, Calendar... just became the foundation of an autonomous agent platform.

What Google Actually Shipped

For years, Google called their AI development platform "Vertex AI."

Nobody outside of engineering teams knew what it was.

Now it's called the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.

And it comes with a no-code agent builder built directly into Google Workspace.

That means if you already use Google for your business... you already have access to this.

No new tools. No new subscriptions. No new logins.

The A2A Protocol Is the Real Story

Here's what actually matters in this announcement.

A2A. Agent-to-Agent protocol.

Google's agents can now communicate with agents from other platforms. That includes Claude. That includes OpenAI tools. That includes anything built on the A2A standard.

For years, your tools have been siloed. Every app lives in its own world. You've been the bridge between all of them... manually copying, pasting, updating.

A2A means agents start doing that bridging work for you.

Picture this.

A new lead fills out your intake form.

The Gmail agent reads it. Passes it to the Calendar agent. Calendar finds an open slot and books the discovery call. Drive agent pulls up your client onboarding template. CRM updates with their details.

You didn't touch any of it.

That's not the future. That's what A2A protocol is designed to enable right now.
