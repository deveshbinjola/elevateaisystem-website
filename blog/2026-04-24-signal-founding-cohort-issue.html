2 Ideas

IDEA 1 · The Sequence

You can't automate a voice you haven't named.

Everyone I see failing with "AI for coaches" skipped the voice codex step. They grabbed a prompt library. Plugged in GPT. Wondered why their content reads like ten thousand other coaches. The sequence matters: systemize → then automate. Name the claim. Capture the cadence. THEN hand it to the agent. Do it backwards and AI flattens your brand instead of amplifying it.

IDEA 2 · Embodiment as Architecture

Your breathwork practice is infrastructure, not aesthetic.

The coaches I watch win with AI aren't "tech-forward." They're body-forward. They've done enough inner work that they can hear when an AI output is off. They have a signal for "not me." That signal is the filter. Without it, the AI flattens you. With it, the AI scales you. Embodiment isn't a personality trait, it's the QA layer on your entire content system.
