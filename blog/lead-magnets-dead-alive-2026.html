03 · The Psychology

Why people actually opt in.

Four triggers drive the decision. The best lead magnets hit at least two simultaneously.

1

Loss Aversion

People are 2x more motivated to avoid losses than to gain. "Stop losing X" beats "Get more X" every time.

2

Identity Resonance

Hooks that reflect who someone IS create instant connection. For coaching, identity hooks outperform benefit hooks consistently.

3

Specificity Bias

Precise, measurable outcomes feel more valuable. "5 questions in 90 seconds" converts 3-5x better than "free guide."

4

Curiosity Gap

Create a gap between what they know and what they could know. "Discover your hidden pattern" compels action.

04 · Hook Formulas

Identity + Specificity = Highest conversion.

For coaching businesses, identity hooks outperform benefit hooks. But the winning formula combines both.

Identity Hook
"For coaches who are done playing small."

Names who they are or want to be. Creates instant "this is for me" signal. Best for niche, values-driven audiences.

Benefit Hook
"Get 30 days of content in 15 minutes."

Shows you understand their struggle and offer a way out. Best for broader audiences, tactical offers.

Loss Aversion Hook
"Stop losing clients to coaches with worse results."

2x more motivating than gain framing. Activates urgency without being salesy. Stings because it's true.

Combined - Highest Converting
"Your content sounds like everyone else's. You don't."

Identity (you're unique) + loss (your content doesn't reflect it) + specificity (implied diagnosis). This formula combines all three triggers at once.

The cheapest conversion lever most coaches skip.

Adding the right social proof is the lowest-effort, highest-impact change across all your lead generation assets.

06 · The Gating Principle

Gate the transformation. Not the information.

The 2026 model: give the diagnosis free, gate the implementation, gate the community behind a paid qualifier.

🔓
Free

The Diagnosis

Quiz results, assessment score, instant insight. Creates the "oh shit" moment that drives action.

✉
Email Gate

The Prescription

Full report, action plan, personalized recommendations. Worth the email because they're already invested.

💳
Paid Gate

The Community + Tools

Quick-start kit, community access, AI-generated content. Filters for buyers and raises lead quality 10x.

By the time someone sees their diagnosis, they're emotionally invested. The email field isn't a wall. It's a door they want to walk through. And a small paid qualifier after that separates tire-kickers from people ready to invest in real transformation.
