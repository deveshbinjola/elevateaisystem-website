Intelligence Just Got Cheap. Your Clarity Didn't.

The Signal: This week Google shipped Gemini 3.5 Flash at roughly $1.50 per million tokens. It runs about 4x faster than last quarter's frontier and it beats the Pro tier it sits below. Read that slowly. The smartest tool on earth now costs less than a vending machine snack to run, and it keeps getting cheaper. Everyone is celebrating. Almost nobody is asking the only question that matters for a coach: when a genius costs pennies, what is actually scarce now?

There's a number from this week that should stop you.

A frontier-class model. $1.50 per million tokens.

Four times faster than the thing everyone was amazed by three months ago.

And it outperforms the more expensive tier sitting right above it.

Most coaches read that and feel a little spike of anxiety.

"It's getting smarter. It's getting cheaper. It's coming for me."

That's the wrong read.

The Price of Smart Is Collapsing

Here's the uncomfortable truth nobody at the conference said into a microphone.

Intelligence is no longer the expensive part.

For a decade, being smart was a moat. You knew the framework. You'd done the reps. People paid you because the knowing was rare and the access was gated.

Now the knowing is a commodity that costs less than your coffee.

That curve only points one direction.

And here's what it means in plain language.

The cost of running an agent that thinks all day, in your method, never sleeps, and answers in your voice... is now rounding error.

The thing you used to charge $2,000 to deliver, the model can deliver for the price of a sandwich.

If your offer is "I know the thing," the floor just dropped out from under it.

So What Actually Got More Valuable?

This is the part the anxiety hides.

When intelligence becomes free and infinite, it doesn't make you worthless.

It makes one specific thing about you worth more than it has ever been.

A point of view.

The model can reason. It can write. It can plan. It can do all of that brilliantly for pennies.

What it cannot do is decide what's worth saying.

It has no stake. No scars. No nine years of getting it wrong before getting it right. No body that tightens when a client is lying to themselves on a call.

It is a perfect, infinite, weightless intelligence with nothing it actually believes.

The Bottleneck Moved. Most Coaches Are Still Guarding the Old One.

For years the constraint was capability. Could you do the thing.

So coaches optimized for capability. More certifications. More frameworks. More "here are 47 ways to use ChatGPT."

That was the right move in 2022.

It's the wrong move now.

Because capability is the part that just went to almost zero.

The new bottleneck is clarity. Having a lens sharp enough that running it through an infinite intelligence produces something only you would have produced.

Competing On Cheap Intelligence

  • "I know the framework, you don't"
  • Stack more tactics, tools and certifications
  • Sell information a $1.50 model now hands out free
  • Sound like every other coach the model trained on
  • Race a machine that just got 4x faster and cheaper

Competing On Scarce Clarity

  • "Here is how I see this, and why"
  • Sharpen one lens until it cuts clean
  • Sell judgment and taste no model can fake
  • Sound unmistakably like you, on purpose
  • Use the cheap genius as an amplifier, not a competitor

The coaches who panic this year will respond by learning more tools.

They'll try to out-know a thing that knows everything.

That's a race you cannot win and never needed to enter.

Cheap Intelligence Is The Best Thing To Ever Happen To A Clear Coach

Flip it around.

If you actually have a point of view... a real one, earned, specific, slightly uncomfortable... then a frontier model at $1.50 a million tokens isn't your replacement.

It's the cheapest, fastest, most tireless distribution engine for your thinking that has ever existed.

You bring the lens. It brings the leverage.

A model can generate a thousand brilliant answers a second. It cannot tell you which one is true for the person in front of you. Cheap intelligence doesn't replace the coach with a point of view. It finally gives that coach unlimited hands.

The coach who knows exactly what they stand for now has an army.

The coach who's been hiding behind borrowed frameworks now has nothing left to hide behind.

The price drop didn't change who wins. It just removed the place the unclear ones were hiding.

Your Move

Stop asking "how do I keep up with the models."

You can't, and you don't need to. They'll be cheaper and faster again next quarter. That's now permanent.

Ask the harder question instead.

If I handed an infinite, free genius to my client tomorrow... what would they still need me for?

Write the answer down in one sentence.

If you can't, that's not a tooling problem. That's a clarity problem. And clarity is the one thing the price drop just made priceless.

If you can, then build the thing that runs your answer at scale.

The intelligence is basically free now.

The point of view is the whole game.

Got the clarity? Let's give it unlimited hands.
