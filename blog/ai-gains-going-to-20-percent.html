75% of AI's Gains Are Going to 20% of Businesses. Here's What They Know That You Don't.

The Signal: PwC's 2026 AI Performance Study (released April 13) surveyed 1,217 executives across 25 sectors. Finding: 74% of AI's economic value is being captured by just 20% of organizations. The top performers aren't using better tools. They're redesigning how they work.

Everyone's using AI now.

That's the thing nobody wants to say out loud.

Your competitor has ChatGPT. So does the coach down the street. So does the person who started last Tuesday.

Access isn't the advantage anymore.

And yet... some businesses are pulling away at a speed that makes no sense.

PwC just explained why.

The 80/20 Split Nobody Expected

PwC dropped their 2026 AI Performance Study today. 1,217 senior executives. 25 industries. And the headline should make every coach stop scrolling.

20%
of companies capture...
74%
of all AI economic value

Twenty percent of businesses are capturing three-quarters of all the economic gains from AI.

The other 80% are splitting the scraps.

This isn't a small gap. This is a canyon. And it's widening every quarter.

What the 20% Do Differently

Here's the part that matters for you.

The top performers aren't using fancier AI models. They're not spending more money on tools. They're not hiring AI engineers.

They're doing one thing the other 80% refuses to do.

They redesign their workflows around AI.

PwC found that companies generating the strongest AI returns are 2x as likely to redesign how they work... rather than just layering AI tools on top of what they were already doing.

Redesign workflows
Top 20%
Just add AI tools
Bottom 80%

Read that again.

The winners aren't better at prompting. They're better at rethinking.

Adding ChatGPT to a broken workflow just gives you a faster broken workflow.

What This Looks Like for Coaches

Most coaches I work with start the same way.

"I use AI to write my captions."

Cool. You're in the 80%.

The 20% coach doesn't use AI to write captions. They built a system where AI writes the caption, schedules it, tracks engagement, identifies which leads engaged, scores those leads, triggers a personalized email sequence, and books the discovery call.

Same starting point. Completely different result.

One added a tool. The other redesigned the workflow.

The Numbers Are Insane

Here's what the "workflow redesign" approach is producing for solopreneurs right now.

A 2026 survey of solopreneurs using AI agent stacks found an average revenue increase of 340% with no corresponding increase in working hours.

And the cost comparison is almost laughable.

That's $12,000 to $48,000 in annual savings. While doing more work. While growing faster.

The people getting these results aren't tech founders. They're coaches. Consultants. Designers. Solo operators who made one decision...

They stopped adding tools and started building systems.

The "Freelance Agentics" Movement

There's a name for what's happening right now.

It's called Freelance Agentics.

Individual specialists using orchestrated AI stacks to do the work of teams 10x their size. Legal professionals. Designers. Consultants. Engineers.

And coaches.

The ones who get this are saving 15 to 20 hours every single week. Not on busy work. On the work that used to feel essential but was actually just... process.

Email follow-ups. Proposal drafting. Content scheduling. Lead scoring. Invoice generation. Client onboarding.

All running. All automated. All while you're coaching.

The Question You Should Be Asking

PwC's single strongest finding?

The number one factor driving AI performance wasn't efficiency. It wasn't cost savings. It wasn't productivity.

It was finding new revenue opportunities.

The 20% are using AI to grow. To build new offers. To reach new clients. To scale in ways that weren't possible when everything required their hands.

The 80% are using AI to save time on tasks they probably shouldn't be doing in the first place.

There's nothing wrong with saving time. But time saved without a growth strategy is just... free time with no direction.

Your Move

Look at your AI usage this week.

Be honest.

Are you adding tools to old workflows? Or are you redesigning how your business actually runs?

Are you in the 20% capturing the gains? Or the 80% wondering why the tools aren't working?

The difference isn't intelligence. It's not budget. It's not tech skills.

It's the willingness to rethink... not just add.

That's the signal in today's data.

And it's the most important question a coach can answer in 2026.
