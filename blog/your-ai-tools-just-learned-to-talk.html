Your AI Tools Just Learned to Talk to Each Other

The Signal: Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) just crossed 97 million installs. OpenAI, Google, Mistral, and Cohere all adopted it. This is the moment AI stops being "tools" and becomes "systems."

Most coaches I work with are drowning in AI tools.

One for content. One for email. One for scheduling. One for leads.

Every tool is smart on its own.

None of them talk to each other.

That just changed.

What Actually Happened

Anthropic created something called the Model Context Protocol. MCP for short.

Think of it like USB for AI.

Before USB, every device needed its own cable. Its own plug. Its own driver. Nothing was compatible with anything.

Then USB showed up and everything just... connected.

MCP does the same thing for AI tools.

Your CRM can talk to your email writer. Your calendar can talk to your content planner. Your lead scoring can talk to your follow-up sequences.

One protocol. One language. Everything connected.

97 million installs in 16 months. For context, React took 3 years to hit 100 million. This is the fastest-adopted AI infrastructure standard in history.

Why Coaches Should Care

Here's the uncomfortable truth.

If you're still using AI tools one at a time... copy-pasting between ChatGPT and your email platform... manually moving data from your intake form to your CRM...

You're using AI like it's 2024.

The coaches who are winning right now? They're not using more tools.

They're building systems where tools work together automatically.

A lead fills out your intake form. Your AI reads it, scores the lead, drafts a personalized follow-up, schedules it at the right time, and updates your CRM.

No copy-pasting. No switching tabs. No manual work.

That's what MCP makes possible.

The Real Shift

This isn't about one tool getting better.

This is about the entire AI ecosystem learning to cooperate.

Every major AI company... OpenAI, Google, Mistral, Cohere... adopted MCP. That almost never happens. Companies this big almost never agree on a standard this fast.

What it means is simple.

The "walled garden" era of AI is ending. The era of connected, intelligent systems is starting.

And the coaches who build those systems first?

They'll have a moat that's almost impossible to copy.

What to Do About It

If you're just getting started with AI

Stop collecting tools. Start thinking in systems. Before you sign up for one more AI subscription, ask yourself: "Does this connect to what I'm already using?"

If you're already using AI tools

Look for MCP-compatible platforms. They'll be the ones that let your tools share context... so your email writer knows what your CRM knows, and your content planner knows what your calendar knows.
