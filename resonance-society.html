Designed for coaches who are already booked. Async-first. Every live touch is recorded so the time zone doesn't trap you.

04 · Questions

Asked before the door opens.

I just finished the Blueprint. Do I really need the Society?

No. The Blueprint is complete on its own. The Society is for the coach who wants the room. If you want the doc and out, take the doc. The Society is the slow rung between the free Blueprint and the $2K Resonance Build, for coaches who'd benefit from the audits and the brothers in between.

How is this different from The Resonance Build ($2K)?

The Build is done-for-you. We install your Blueprint into a live system in thirty days. The Society is done-with-you. You stay at the wheel, with a room and a coach behind you. Members of the Society skip the public Build application and get direct kickoff access when they're ready.

What's the founding rate, and how long does it hold?

$99/month, locked for the life of your membership. Standard rate at full launch will be $149/month. Founding seats are capped at the first 25, then we close the founding tier permanently.

What if it doesn't fit?

First month is free if you cancel inside the first 30 days. After that, cancel anytime, one click, no calls. We'd rather you leave clean than stay stuck.

Where does the room live?

The Resonance Room on Skool. Free tier is open to anyone who finishes the Blueprint. The Society is the paid layer of that room, with the teardowns, audits, and direct Build access.

Why a paid community when you have a free one?

Because the free room is a top-of-funnel asset and the paid room is a working room. Different jobs. Greg Eisenberg's Community Empire is the reference model: free for warming, $99/mo for the people who actually want to build. Both are needed.
