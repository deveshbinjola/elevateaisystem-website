Questions

Frequently Asked

What exactly are the AI agents?

Custom automation workflows trained on your voice and brand. They run on platforms like Paperclip, n8n, or Make, connected to your website, social accounts, email provider, and calendar. Think of them as a team that works 24/7 and sounds exactly like you.

How long does the whole process take?

Foundation: 3 weeks. Launch: 4 weeks. Visibility: 5 weeks + 3 months ongoing. We build fast but never rush, your brand and agents need to be right.

Will the agents actually sound like me?

Yes. During discovery, we record how you naturally talk about your work. We extract your verbal patterns, favourite phrases, and sentence rhythm. Every agent is trained on this voice profile. We test with 3 sample outputs per agent and refine until you can't tell the difference.

What do I need to provide?

Just yourself. Show up to the discovery session, give us access to your accounts, and provide feedback on drafts and agent outputs. We handle all the building, connecting, and testing.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. 2-payment plans (50/50) on all tiers. 3-payment plans (40/30/30) on Visibility. Monthly payment plans also available with a small premium.

What happens after the project is done?

You own everything, the brand, the website, and all agent configurations. Foundation and Launch clients can continue with our Ongoing Growth plan ($197/month) for agent maintenance and content support. Visibility clients get 3 months included, then can continue monthly.

What is The ELEVATE Method?

Our 4-pillar search visibility framework: SEO (Google rankings), AEO (AI Overviews and Featured Snippets), GEO (Google AI generative citations), and LLMO (ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude recommendations). Most agencies only do SEO. We optimize for all four.

What if I already have a website?

We can rebuild or optimize what you have. During the discovery session, we'll assess whether a refresh or full rebuild makes more sense. Either way, your AI agents get connected to whatever platform you're on.
