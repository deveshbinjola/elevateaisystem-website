Questions

Frequently asked

What does Soma replace?

Soma replaces your CRM (HoneyBook, Dubsado), email marketing (ConvertKit, ActiveCampaign), funnel builder (ClickFunnels, Leadpages), content tools (Canva, Later, Loom), and scheduling (Calendly, Acuity) with one connected system. The average coach saves $120-270/month by consolidating to Soma.

How is Soma different from Kajabi or GoHighLevel?

Kajabi and GoHighLevel are horizontal platforms built for everyone. Soma is vertical, built specifically for embodiment coaches by an embodiment coach. The biggest difference: Soma has a conversational AI interface (you talk to it instead of clicking through dashboards) and Session Intelligence that makes you a better coach over time. No other platform offers that.

What is Session Intelligence?

Session Intelligence runs silently during your coaching sessions, no bots join the call, no intrusion. It captures notes, tracks client commitments, and generates pre-session briefs so you never walk into a call cold. Over time, it recognizes patterns across your clients and surfaces insights that sharpen your coaching practice. Available on the Pro plan ($499/month).

Does AI write my content?

Soma's Brand OS trains on YOUR actual content, your posts, talks, and writing. It learns your voice, your metaphors, your way of seeing the world. Then it drafts content that sounds like you on your best day. You bring the ideas and creativity. Soma handles the production. Every piece still gets your review before it goes out.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Start free, no credit card required. Explore Soma Voice, set up your CRM, and see if it fits before you pay anything. Plans start at $79/month when you're ready to upgrade.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. That's the point. You talk to Soma, you don't configure it. Ask it to draft a post, check your leads, or prep for a session. If you can have a conversation, you can use Soma. For coaches who want full backend control, Soma Admin is there, but most coaches never need it.
