Common questions
Frequently Asked Questions
What is an AI systems audit?

A review of how your business actually runs: operations, workflows, tech stack, data, and goals. It identifies exactly where AI and automation save time and recover money, and where they don't belong. You get a custom AI opportunity map, not a generic checklist.

Who is this AI audit designed for?

Two kinds of businesses: multi-site operators such as treatment centers, clinics and service businesses, and coaches or consultants doing $10K+ per month. If the work that isn't your real work is eating your week, this is for you.

How long does the audit take?

The intake form takes about 10 minutes. After you submit, we prepare your AI opportunity map and schedule strategy calls, all within the first 14 days.

What does the audit cost?

Pricing is flat and quoted before you commit, on a free call, once we both know the scope. The audit covers a deep-dive analysis of your operations, workflows, tech stack, and goals, plus a custom AI opportunity map and strategy calls to walk through it. If we go on to build, the audit is credited toward the build.

What do I get after the audit?

You receive a custom AI opportunity map that identifies which workflows can be automated, which tools to use, where your biggest time and revenue leaks are, and a prioritized action plan. Plus strategy calls to walk through everything.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

Not at all. This audit is designed for coaches who are great at coaching but want help figuring out the AI and systems side. We translate the technical into the practical, you just need to answer honestly about your business.
