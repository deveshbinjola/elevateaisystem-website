Self-Diagnostic

Can you answer "yes" to all of these?

SEO
Does Google show your site when someone searches for what you do?
If your title tags say "Home" or just your name, Google doesn't know what you offer. You're invisible to anyone searching for your services.
SEO
Are all your pages indexed by Google?
Most small businesses have 50-80% of their pages missing from Google's index. If Google can't see your pages, neither can your clients.
AEO
Does your content appear in Google's AI Overview answers?
When someone asks Google a question in your niche, AI Overviews pull answers from websites with structured, Q&A-formatted content. No structure = no citation.
AEO
Do you own any Featured Snippets for your key topics?
Featured Snippets appear above all organic results. They're captured by pages that answer questions directly in 40-60 words under clear headings.
GEO
Does Google's AI summary mention your business by name?
Google AI Overviews now generate summaries that cite specific businesses. Getting cited requires entity clarity, structured data, and trust signals most sites lack.
GEO
Can AI crawlers actually access your website?
Many websites block AI crawlers (Google-Extended, PerplexityBot) without realizing it. If they can't read your site, they can't recommend your business.
LLMO
If someone asks ChatGPT to recommend a business like yours, does it mention you?
ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude each cite different sources. Getting recommended requires being mentioned on the specific platforms each AI prefers — Reddit, directories, authority blogs.
LLMO
Is your business mentioned on Reddit, Clutch, G2, or industry forums?
Perplexity pulls 47% of its citations from Reddit. ChatGPT favors directory sites like G2 and Clutch. If you're not on these platforms, AI chatbots literally can't find you.

If you answered "no" to any of these — that's exactly what our free audit uncovers.
