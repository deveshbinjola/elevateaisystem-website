Technologies This Week

AI agents can now operate your CRM

New "computer use" capabilities mean AI can click, type, and navigate your screen — managing your pipeline, posting content, and handling workflows without custom integrations. Early, but it changes the automation game.

Ideas You Can Use This Week

Turn your FAQ into a lead magnet quiz

You already answer the same 10 questions on every sales call. Turn them into a self-assessment quiz — each question maps to a pain point, the results page positions your offer as the solution. Takes 2 hours to build, runs forever.

Let AI write your email nurture sequence in one sitting

Paste your top 5 testimonials into Claude. Ask it to extract the transformation pattern, then draft a 5-email sequence that mirrors that journey — pain point to realization to action. Edit for voice, schedule, done.

Tool of the Week

Descript — Edit video by editing text

Record a coaching Q&A, drop it in Descript, and edit the transcript like a doc. Delete a sentence, delete that part of the video. Removes filler words automatically. Cuts editing time by 80% for coaches making video content.
