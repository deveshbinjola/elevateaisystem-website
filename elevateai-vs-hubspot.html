Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use both Elevate AI and HubSpot together?

Yes. Many coaches use Elevate AI System to generate leads and content, then export contacts into HubSpot if they have a sales team. Our system feeds leads into HubSpot's CRM. It's not either/or—it's infrastructure + CRM.

What if I start with HubSpot and want to switch later?

Your contacts export easily. But you'll have wasted 80-150 hours building systems we'd have built for you. Many coaches find it cheaper to switch to Elevate AI System than to continue the DIY path—you recover the time investment immediately.

Does Elevate AI replace my sales process?

No. We build the lead generation and nurturing system. You still run discovery calls and close. We're the infrastructure that gets qualified leads to your door. Many coaches then use HubSpot's CRM to track their sales pipeline alongside our system.

What's the difference in lead quality?

Elevate AI generates leads from organic search (Google, AI Overviews, LLMO) and email nurture sequences—typically higher quality because they've self-qualified through content. HubSpot captures leads from forms but doesn't generate or nurture them automatically. Lead quality depends on your overall marketing, not the CRM.

Can I build an Elevate AI-like system using HubSpot?

Theoretically, yes. You'd need: SEO content writer ($2K-5K/month), email sequences designer, setup consultant, ongoing optimization. Total: $60-100K per year. Elevate AI costs $4,500-60,000/year and includes all of it. The comparison shows why coaches choose done-for-you.
