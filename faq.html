About & Framework

ElevateAI Systems builds AI-powered marketing systems for coaches, consultants, creators, and service businesses. We help you get found across Google, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude using our 4-pillar framework: SEO, AEO, GEO, and LLMO.
The ELEVATE Method is our proprietary 4-pillar search visibility framework: SEO (Google rankings), AEO (AI Overviews and Featured Snippets), GEO (Google generative search citations), and LLMO (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude recommendations). Most businesses only optimize for SEO. The ELEVATE Method ensures you're visible across every modern search surface, not just Google.
LLMO is the practice of optimizing your online presence so that AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini cite, recommend, or reference your business when users ask relevant questions. As more people search using AI instead of Google, LLMO is rapidly becoming as important as traditional SEO. It involves LLM seeding through Reddit, Medium, directories, podcast appearances, and high-authority content.
AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It's the practice of optimizing content to appear in Google's Featured Snippets, AI Overviews, and People Also Ask boxes. AEO requires direct-answer content formatting, FAQ schema markup, question-based headings, and authoritative concise responses. It's Pillar 2 of The ELEVATE Method.
GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization, optimizing your website and content to be cited by Google's AI-generated summaries and AI Overviews. GEO requires entity clarity, structured data, strong E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), original data, and clear topical authority. It's Pillar 3 of The ELEVATE Method.
