## Post 1 — From: "Why Most Coaching Websites Don't Rank on Google"

Most coaching websites are invisible to Google.

Not because the content is bad — because the technical foundation is broken.

I've audited dozens of coaching sites. These 5 mistakes show up almost every time:

1. No keyword strategy — you write about "transformational coaching" when people search "how to get unstuck in my career"
2. Duplicate or missing meta tags — Google can't understand your pages
3. Zero internal linking — your pages are islands that don't connect
4. Slow load times — heavy page builders are killing your mobile rankings
5. No fresh content — a static 5-page site tells Google you're abandoned

The fix for all 5? It's not expensive. It's intentional.

SEO isn't optional for coaches who want to stop paying for ads forever.

6 months from now, you'll either have organic traffic — or you'll still be wondering why nobody finds you.

## Post 2 — From: "How AI Is Changing the Game for Coaching Businesses"

AI won't replace coaches.

But coaches who use AI will replace coaches who don't.

Here's the honest breakdown — no hype, no BS:

What AI actually does well for coaches:
→ Turns 1 long-form piece into 20+ social posts
→ Automates email follow-up sequences
→ Scores and qualifies leads before you reach out
→ Schedules weeks of content in hours

What AI still can't do:
→ Build genuine human connection
→ Read a room during a coaching call

The coaches who figure this out first win. Not because AI makes them better coaches — because it gives them 10-15 hours/week back to actually coach.

Here's the trap though: don't try to implement everything at once.

Pick the task that eats the most of your time. Automate that first. For most coaches, that's content creation.

In 12 months, the gap between AI-equipped coaches and everyone else will be enormous.

## Post 3 — From: "The Coach's Guide to Getting Clients Without Cold DMs"

Cold DMs have a 1-2% conversion rate.

That means 100 messages for 1-2 "no thanks" replies.

And you're damaging your brand every time you send one.

Here's what actually works instead:

The Attract Model — a system where clients come to you:

Step 1: Publish consistently on 1-2 platforms (not 7)
Step 2: Create content that answers what your ideal client is Googling
Step 3: Build a lead magnet that solves a specific problem
Step 4: Capture emails with a landing page
Step 5: Nurture with a 5-7 email sequence that builds trust
Step 6: They book the discovery call themselves

The coaches who are consistently booked aren't chasing leads.

They built a system that runs whether they're working or sleeping.

"Free guide to success" won't convert.
"The 7-Day Plan to Land Your First 3 High-Ticket Clients" will.

Specificity wins every time.

## Post 4 — From: "Coaches Don't Need More Content — They Need a System"

You don't need another reel.

You need to call the 32 people who ghosted you.

Harsh? Maybe. But here's the math:

Most coaches are addicted to posting. But content without systems is noise.

Before you write another caption, you need:

A lead database — not a mental list. A real CRM tracking every prospect, where they came from, and when you last spoke.

A follow-up rhythm — 80% of sales happen after the 5th touchpoint. Most coaches give up after 1. That's where your money is hiding.

A real SEO strategy — social media posts die in 24 hours. Blog posts that rank bring leads for years.

Weekly problem-driven writing — not motivational quotes. Content that positions you as the obvious expert.

Here's the test: Can you clearly state the top 3 problems you solve — in the words your clients would use, not coaching jargon?

If not, more content won't save you.

Content is a multiplier. If you multiply zero clarity by 1,000 posts, you still get zero.

## Post 5 — From: "Top 5 Mistakes Costing Your Coaching Business Thousands"

A coach charging $3,000/client who closes just 2 more clients per month from better follow-up adds $72,000/year.

That's the cost of NOT following up.

Here are 5 mistakes I see in almost every coaching business:

1. No follow-up system — 80% of sales need 5+ touchpoints. You stop at 1.

2. Undercharging — You charge $150/hr for coaching that helps clients make $50K more/year. Price on outcomes, not hours.

3. No lead generation system — "Post and hope" isn't a strategy. You need attract → capture → nurture.

4. Trying to be everywhere — 5+ platforms = mediocre on all of them. Go deep on 1-2.

5. A website that reads like a resume — "I'm passionate about helping people" doesn't convert. Name their pain, show the transformation, add proof.

These mistakes compound. Stack all 5 and you're leaving $100K+ on the table annually.

But the flip side is also true — fix all 5 and the growth compounds in your favor.
