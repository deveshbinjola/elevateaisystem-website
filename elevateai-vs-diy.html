When DIY Actually Makes Sense

Scenario 1: You're Pre-Revenue (No Clients Yet)

If you have zero paying clients, your hourly rate is technically infinite (you're not billing anyone). DIY makes sense because your time is "free." Build the system while you're bootstrapping. But be honest: will you actually finish it, or will it sit half-done?

Scenario 2: You Love the Technical Side

Some coaches genuinely enjoy marketing setup and optimization. If that's you, DIY can be rewarding. Just make sure you're willing to dive deep into SEO, email marketing, automation, and analytics. It's a real skill set.

Scenario 3: You Have Plenty of Time & Budget Constraints

If you have 80+ hours to invest and need to minimize monthly costs, DIY works. But you'll be slow to market. First leads: 3-6 months. First revenue: 6-9 months. Can you wait that long?

Scenario 4: You Want to Learn Marketing

If education is the goal, DIY is perfect. Build a system, make mistakes, learn what works. By year 2, you'll be an expert. But this is expensive education—could cost $15K-30K to learn what we already know.

When DIY Does NOT Make Sense

❌ You're Already Booked with Clients

If you have paying clients, your time is expensive ($100-500/hr). Every hour you spend on DIY setup is revenue lost. Hire out. Let us do it in weeks while you focus on coaching and revenue.

❌ You're Trying to Hit Revenue Goals

DIY will delay your timeline by 3-6 months. If you're targeting $100K revenue by year-end, DIY might cost you that goal entirely. Done-for-you gets you there faster.

❌ You Have a High Hourly Rate

If you coach at $200-500/hr, DIY doesn't make financial sense. You're paying $15K-30K in lost coaching hours to save $2K-5K in agency fees. That's backwards.

❌ You've Never Done Marketing Before

The learning curve alone will kill you. Tool selection, setup, troubleshooting, optimization... this is 4-6 months of trial and error. An expert does this in 2 weeks with predictable results.

The Decision Framework: Time vs Money

Choose DIY If:

  • ✓ Your hourly coaching rate is under $75/hr
  • ✓ You have 80+ hours free in the next 90 days
  • ✓ You have zero paying clients (your time is "free")
  • ✓ You enjoy technical setup & learning
  • ✓ You can wait 3-6 months for first results
  • ✓ Your goal is education, not immediate revenue

Choose Elevate AI If:

  • ✓ Your hourly rate is $100-500/hr
  • ✓ You want results in weeks, not months
  • ✓ You have paying clients (or a revenue goal)
  • ✓ You don't want to learn SEO/email/marketing
  • ✓ You want predictable lead flow
  • ✓ You prefer to focus on coaching, not setup
