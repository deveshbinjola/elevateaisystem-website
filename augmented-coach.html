The real questions, answered straight.

How is this different from /brand-os?
They're the same program. /brand-os zooms in on Week 1, The Positioning Drop, because that's the hook most people come in through. /augmented-coach (this page) shows the full 8-week arc. Same price. Same seats. Same cohort.
What's the weekly time commitment?
Week 1 is minimal on your end, you fill out a 30-min intake form and show up to one 60-min handoff call. Weeks 2-8: one 90-min live call, plus ~4-6 hours of integration work per week. Most seats report 6-8 hrs/week total.
What if I can't make every call live?
All calls are recorded and dropped into your portal same day. The Signal channel is async-first. First 2 misses get a 30-min 1:1 makeup. If you're going to miss 3+ live sessions I'd push you to Batch 2, this cohort is small enough that presence compounds.
Why small (10 seats) instead of 50-100?
Because I'm the bottleneck, and I chose to be. The Drop is solo founder work. The cohort calls only work if everyone gets real air time. Bigger rooms turn into courses. This isn't a course.
What do I actually walk away with?
A full brand book HTML. A running AI agent in your stack. A funnel with traffic. A voice codex. A content system that produces 5 platform-native assets from one idea. A small room of 10 peers. And the clarity to stop renting your positioning from ChatGPT.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. Two payments of $997 over 30 days, or a single $1,997 founding payment. Both are on the Stripe Payment Link you'll receive after acceptance.
When does Batch 1 start and finish?
Drops run May 5-10, 2026. First cohort call is the week of May 12. Cohort wraps July 7. Batch 2 opens around September 2026.
Why "men's coaches" specifically?
Because that's who I serve best, embodied men running $10K+/mo practices. The cohort is open to adjacent operators too (men's therapists, creators in masculine work, some men's circle leaders). If you're a woman coach, I have referrals, the work is real, but this isn't my room.
