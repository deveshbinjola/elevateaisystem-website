3   Technologies This Week
Automation

Claude can now use your computer

Anthropic launched "computer use" — Claude can click, type, and navigate your screen like a human would. For coaches, this means AI can now operate your CRM, post content, and manage workflows without needing custom integrations built first. It's still early, but this changes what's possible with automation at the ground level.

Google's NotebookLM now generates audio overviews

Upload your course material or coaching framework, and NotebookLM generates a podcast-style audio summary — two AI hosts discussing your content. Coaches can turn their intellectual property into audio in minutes. If you've been putting off a podcast, this is your shortcut to repurposing existing material without recording a single episode.

2   Ideas You Can Use This Week

Turn your FAQ into a lead magnet quiz

You already answer the same 10 questions on every sales call — you're just doing it one person at a time. Turn those into a self-assessment quiz: each question maps to a pain point, and the results page positions your offer as the solution. Takes about 2 hours to build using Typeform or Scorapp, runs forever, and filters your leads before they even book a call.

Write your entire email nurture sequence in one sitting

Paste your top 5 client testimonials into Claude and ask it to extract the transformation pattern — what they felt before, the moment things shifted, what they have now. Then ask it to draft a 5-email nurture sequence that mirrors that journey from pain point to decision. Edit for your voice, plug it into your email platform, done. Most coaches have the raw material sitting in their inbox already.
