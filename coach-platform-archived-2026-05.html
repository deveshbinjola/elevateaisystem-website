The honest questions

If your gut hesitated. Here's the rest.

What does "voice-native" actually mean?
You spend ~45 minutes with the Brand OS Agent on your first session — it interviews you and produces a voice profile (tone, sentence rhythm, vocabulary you use, vocabulary you'd never use, opener phrases, closer phrases). Every AI feature in Coach Platform pulls from that profile. So when an auto-response goes out, it doesn't sound like a generic AI assistant — it sounds like a message you'd actually send. Most users tell us their drafts feel "spookily accurate" by message 5.
What does "Founding 10 free" really mean?
During the build phase (roughly the next 6 months), the first 10 coaches accepted pay $0. You get every feature as it ships. When public pricing launches ($497 setup + $100/mo), Founding 10 lock in $97/mo forever — no setup fee, ever. The trade is: you give me your honest feedback weekly, and you let me reference your usage patterns when I sell to coaches 11+. That's the deal.
Does this replace my CRM?
Honest answer: it might, but that's not the pitch. The pitch is that it sits in front of whatever you use today and does three things your CRM doesn't — voice-native auto-response, focus queue, and reach tracking. Some coaches keep Notion or HubSpot for archive purposes and run Coach Platform as their daily driver. Some drop the CRM entirely after month two. Both are fine.
Will this work if I don't already have a Brand OS?
Yes — the Brand OS Agent IS your first session. You don't need to have done it before. Most Founding 10 coaches show up without a brand document and walk out 45 minutes later with one. That voice profile is what makes everything else work.
What happens to my data if I leave?
You export everything — leads, messages, voice profile, settings — as a single JSON download. We delete the rest within 30 days. No vendor lock-in, no held-hostage exports.
Why men's coaches specifically?
Because that's who I serve and where the voice profile work is sharpest. Coach Platform works for any coach — but the Founding 10 spots are reserved for men's coaches and adjacent operators ($10K+/mo) so I can keep the cohort small enough to be useful. Batch 2 will open to a wider ICP. If you're a women's coach interested, I have referrals to the right rooms.
How is this different from the Augmented Coach cohort?
Different things. Augmented Coach is an 8-week program where I teach you the system. Coach Platform is the tool that runs the system after the program ends. Most cohort grads end up on Coach Platform because the system needs a substrate. You don't have to do the cohort to get on the platform — but if you've done the cohort, the platform feels like coming home.
What if I'm already happy with GoHighLevel?
Stay there. If GHL is working for you, switching costs aren't worth it. Coach Platform is for coaches who feel the gap I described above — slow response, no voice, too many tabs — and want a sharper, smaller tool that does fewer things deeper. If GHL is paying for itself in your funnel, don't fix what isn't broken.
