# Twitter/X Templates for ElevateAI

## Single Tweet Formula

A single tweet is your chance to capture attention in 280 characters. No second chances, no retweets needed.

### Structure: Hook + Insight + Implied CTA

- **Line 1 (Hook)**: Bold claim, surprising stat, or counterintuitive take
- **Line 2 (Insight)**: One sentence that explains why it matters or what's different
- **Line 3 (Proof or Action)**: Optional—backs up the claim with a number, example, or next step

### Character Limits
- MUST be under 280 characters (including spaces)
- Leave room for hashtags on a second line
- Do NOT use hashtags inside the tweet body

### Example Templates

**Contrarian Take:**
```
You don't need more content.
You need distribution.

Most coaches create in a vacuum.
The ones making $50K+/month have a system for getting found.
```

**Stat-Driven:**
```
83% of coaches' content never gets seen.

The reason? No AI content distribution.

We changed that.
```

**Bold Claim + Why:**
```
SEO is dead for coaches.

Actually, no. Coach SEO is just different.
It's not about keywords.
It's about being found by people ready to buy.
```

**Result-Focused:**
```
$124K in client revenue from 1 content system.

The system? ELEVATE Method.
SEO + AEO + GEO + LLMO.
```

**Question That Implies a Better Way:**
```
Why are you still cold DMing?

If your website was found by 50 qualified leads per week, you wouldn't need to.
```

### What Works
- Start with a stat, question, or contrarian claim
- One insight that builds credibility
- Link to the blog post (always)
- 2-3 hashtags only, on their own line below

### What Doesn't Work
- "Here's the thing:" openers
- Threads disguised as single tweets ("1/" or "Thread:")
- Emoji-heavy formatting
- Generic motivation
- More than one call to action
- Unclear who should care

## Twitter Thread Formula

A thread is 5-7 connected tweets. Each tweet must stand alone as valuable, but together they build a narrative.

### Structure: Hook → Value (4-5 tweets) → CTA

**Tweet 1 (The Hook):**
This is the only tweet people see. It must make them click "Show more" or feel so intrigued they have to read the next one.

Choose ONE hook type:
- **Surprising stat**: "X% of coaches don't realize..."
- **Bold contrarian claim**: "You're doing content wrong."
- **Result/proof**: "$83K in 62 days. Here's exactly how."
- **Question that implies a better way**: "Why are you still cold DMing?"
- **Story setup**: "I fired my copywriter and hired an AI. Here's what happened."

Good examples:
```
I stopped posting content for 30 days.

My leads went up 40%.

Here's why—and how you can do it too.
```

```
Most coaches think distribution is luck.

It's not. It's a system.

Here's the one I built at ElevateAI.
```

```
Your content is invisible to AI.

I can prove it.

Let me show you how to fix it in 3 steps.
```

**Tweets 2-5 (Value):**
Each tweet delivers ONE insight. Together, they build on each other, but each can stand alone.

Rules for value tweets:
- One idea per tweet
- Specific examples or numbers when possible
- Reference frameworks (ELEVATE Method, Content OS, etc.) naturally
- Use short sentences
- Transition phrases sparingly ("Here's the thing:" used once maximum in the thread)
- Include frameworks: "The ELEVATE Method has 4 pillars..."
- Show patterns: "Every coach I work with makes this mistake..."
- Use numbers: "73% of coaches skip this step..."

Good value tweet structures:
```
Most coaches chase vanity metrics.

They care about likes and follows.

But coaches with $50K+/month revenue?
They care about one number: how many qualified leads walked in.
```

```
The secret isn't better content.

It's better distribution.

We built a system that gets content in front of people actively searching for what you teach.
```

```
Here's what changed everything for our clients:

They stopped writing for engagement.
They started writing for lead generation.

One small mindset shift.
Massive results.
```

**Tweet 6 (Closing):**
Summarize the key takeaway and include the blog link.

```
This isn't complex.

It's ELEVATE: SEO + AEO + GEO + LLMO.

It's how we help coaches get from $10K to $50K+/month.

Full breakdown: elevateaisystem.com/blog/[slug]
```

OR

```
The coaches winning right now aren't smarter.

They have a system.

Let me show you the exact framework we use with every client.

Read the full post: elevateaisystem.com/blog/[slug]
```

**Tweet 7 (Optional CTA):**
Only if the value is dense and needs a second CTA:

```
Want to see this system in action?

Join The 3-2-1 newsletter where we share the exact frameworks, tools, and strategies we use to help coaches scale.

Each week: frameworks you can steal, systems you can implement, insights from the trenches.
```

### Thread Rules (Don't Break These)

- **No "Thread:" prefix** — it's redundant and looks amateur
- **No "1/" at the start** — Twitter threads don't need numbering
- **Don't say "Let me explain..."** — just explain
- **Don't apologize for threading** — "Sorry for the long thread" wastes a tweet
- **Keep it tight** — 5-7 tweets max (7 is pushing it)
- **Each tweet stands alone** — someone who only reads tweet 3 should get value
- **Build on each other** — tweet 2 should feel connected to tweet 1
- **End with a hook** — the final tweet should make them want to read the blog

### What Makes a Good Thread
- Starts with a stat, question, or bold claim
- Each tweet delivers new information
- Uses concrete examples, not abstractions
- References frameworks when relevant
- Ends with a clear link to the full post
- No buzzwords or corporate jargon
- Feels like thinking out loud, not a pitch

### What Makes a Bad Thread
- Starts with "Thread:" or "Let me explain"
- Each tweet restates the same idea
- Vague insights ("Work on yourself", "Be authentic")
- Forced emojis or ASCII art
- Long paragraphs in tweet format
- Obvious selling (every tweet pitches something)
- Doesn't deliver on the hook's promise

## Micro-Value Tweet Formula

Micro-value tweets are standalone insights extracted from the blog. They live in the gaps between big content drops.

### What They Are
- Short, punchy insights that feel original
- Not summaries of the blog post
- Not teasers for the blog post
- Pure insight delivered in one tweet
- Should feel like a spontaneous observation

### Formula: Bold Statement + Brief Explanation

Structure:
```
[Bold statement].
[One-sentence explanation or example].
```

Examples:
```
Most coaches write content for Google.

The ones making $50K+/month write content for people.

It's a different skill.
```

```
Your website doesn't convert because it's not optimized for AI discovery.

AI reads differently than humans.
It doesn't care about your story.
It cares about your expertise.
```

```
Content is a commodity.

Distribution is the moat.

This is why most coaches fail—and why some scale to $100K+/month.
```

```
You don't need more followers.

You need followers who are ready to hire.

One is earned through systems.
The other through luck.
```

### Writing Micro-Value Tweets
- Start with a contrarian take or surprising insight
- One example or proof point
- No hashtags in the body (put them below)
- Under 280 characters
- Should feel like the kind of thing people screenshot and share

### Where They Come From
- Extract insights from the blog post that aren't the main point
- Find frameworks mentioned in passing and expand them
- Pull out surprising statistics or results
- Find counterintuitive takes
- These are the "hits" that get retweeted and build authority

### What NOT to do
- Don't tease the blog post ("Wait till you see this post about...")
- Don't use emojis
- Don't make them too complex (one idea only)
- Don't plagiarize from the blog (reword the insight)
- Don't make them generic ("Work hard and you'll win")

## Hashtag Strategy for Twitter

### Rules
- Use 2-3 hashtags ONLY
- Put them on a separate line below the tweet
- Do NOT include hashtags in the tweet body
- Rotate hashtags to avoid looking robotic

### Hashtag Sets for ElevateAI

**Always Available (rotate these in most tweets):**
- #CoachingBusiness
- #AIMarketing
- #ContentMarketing
- #LeadGeneration

**Topic-Specific (use when relevant):**

- Growth content: #Growth #GrowthMarketing

## Cadence & Distribution Tips

- **Single tweets**: 2-3x per week (when you have a hot take or stat)
- **Threads**: 1x per week (usually connected to a new blog post)
- **Micro-value tweets**: Daily or every other day (fill the gaps)
- **Best times**: 9 AM, 1 PM, 5 PM (EST) for coaching audience
- **Retweet your own**: Retweet threads 3-7 days after posting (people miss them)
- **Engage**: Reply to comments on your threads (signals = distribution boost)
