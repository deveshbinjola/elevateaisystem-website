Shared Component, navbar.js
A vanilla JS IIFE that injects the nav bar (CSS + HTML) into every page at runtime. Used by 14 of 17 HTML pages. Three product pages (lead-os, content-os, growth-engine) use custom inline navs with product-specific CTAs. Includes desktop dropdown menu, mobile hamburger, and scroll shadow effect.
