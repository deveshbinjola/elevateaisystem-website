The hidden cost of DIY marketing for coaches

DIY marketing feels free. It isn't. Every hour you spend writing blog posts, figuring out SEO, scheduling LinkedIn content, and learning how Google works is an hour you're not coaching, not selling, and not delivering results for clients.

If your hourly value as a coach is $150-$500, spending 15 hours a week on marketing costs you $2,250-$7,500 in opportunity cost every week. That's before you factor in the tools you subscribe to (Canva, Buffer, SurferSEO, Mailchimp, Apollo...) that add up to $300-500/month without necessarily working together.

The pattern we see: Coaches who DIY their marketing produce great content for 6 weeks, burn out, go quiet for 2 months, restart with a new strategy, and repeat. The algorithm rewards consistency, not sprints.

Is DIY ever the right answer?

Yes, at the very start. If you're a brand-new coach still figuring out your niche and offer, DIY forces you to learn what resonates with your audience. That's valuable. But once your offer is validated and you're trying to scale past $5K/month, DIY becomes the ceiling rather than the path.

The coaches we work with typically come to us after 12-18 months of DIY that produced some results but not the consistency or volume they needed to grow predictably.
