The real cost of hiring a marketing agency as a coach

Marketing agencies built for e-commerce brands, SaaS companies, or large enterprises don't automatically understand coaching. They understand deliverables. You'll often spend the first 3 months educating them on your niche, your audience, and what "results" actually mean in a coaching context — while paying full retainer the whole time.

A typical coaching-focused marketing agency engagement looks like this: $3,000–$8,000/month retainer, a 6-month minimum contract, a 4–8 week onboarding, and then 2–3 months of "strategy" before anything ships. By month 4, you've spent $20,000+ and haven't booked a single client from their work.

The core problem: agencies are built for volume clients. You're one account. Their best strategists are on their $15K/month accounts. You get the junior team.

When should you hire an agency instead?

Be fair: agencies make sense in specific scenarios. If you're running paid social ads at $50K+/month, managing a complex multi-brand content operation, or need broadcast PR and media buying — a full-service agency has the human infrastructure for that. But if you're a coach doing $10K–$100K/month who needs more leads, better content, and Google visibility — you don't need an agency. You need a system.
